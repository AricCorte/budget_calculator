How BudgetFlow calculates it

1. Monthly income − savings = money available for spending.

2. Normal daily allowance = available money ÷ number of days in the month.

3. Every Sunday gets your normal allowance + Sunday extra.

4. If you overspend, the negative amount becomes tomorrow's debt.

5. If you underspend, the leftover stays with you; it does not disappear.

Example: ₹170 daily allowance − ₹180 spent = −₹10. Tomorrow: ₹170 − ₹10 debt = ₹160 usable. If tomorrow is Sunday and Sunday extra is ₹500, it becomes ₹660.
